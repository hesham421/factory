**PROPOSAL 1 — priority scheme (no priority is stated anywhere in the inputs)**
- Affects: all seven stories.
- Options: **A)** every story `—` (nothing stated → nothing invented). **B)** the four vision operations HIGH, the enabling stories MEDIUM. **C)** everything HIGH.
- Researched: the engine allows a priority "only if stated or **clearly implied**". The vision states the module *is* exactly those four operations ("one entity, four operations … one screen"), so their priority is implied by the scope being nothing else; US-NOTE-005 (reaching a deactivated note) and US-NOTE-007 (self-registration) are enabling, not named capabilities. `[KB:erp-domain-standards §4]` treats screen+permission as the delivery unit, which makes US-NOTE-006 as load-bearing as the operations.
- **Recommended: B** — HIGH for US-NOTE-001…004 and US-NOTE-006, MEDIUM for US-NOTE-005 and US-NOTE-007. Success metric stays `—` everywhere: none of the inputs states one, and inventing one would be unsourced.

**PROPOSAL 2 — can the owner reach a note after deactivating it?**
- Affects: US-NOTE-004, US-NOTE-005.
- Options: **A)** no path in v1 — a deactivated note becomes invisible to its owner; only the audit trail keeps it. **B)** the owner needs a deliberate way to see their deactivated notes (need level; P1 decides whether that is a filter, a toggle or a separate view).
- Researched: POL-NOTE-004 says "excluded from its owner's **default** search" — the word *default* only carries meaning if a non-default path exists. POL-NOTE-003 retires by deactivation "alone" precisely so the content survives, and `[KB:erp-domain-standards §6]` makes soft delete the platform default, where the row remains addressable. Option A would make deactivate indistinguishable from delete for the only person allowed to see the note, contradicting the rationale recorded in POL-NOTE-003.
- **Recommended: B**, as a separate story so P1 owns the mechanism — and explicitly *not* a second screen (module-registry SCOPE: one composite screen). If round 2 prefers it folded into US-NOTE-004, I accept that with no loss of coverage; POL-NOTE-004 stays traced either way.

**PROPOSAL 3 — the role name in the stories**
- Affects: all stories.
- Options: **A)** "a user". **B)** "a Notes-screen user" (a user granted the one permission set) / "the note's owner" where ownership is the point. **C)** "an employee".
- Researched: `profiles/erp.yaml → conventions.security_model` grants VIEW/CREATE/UPDATE/DELETE on a *granted screen*, and POL-NOTE-006 makes that grant the single gate for all four operations; the module registry never names an organizational role, so "employee" would be invented vocabulary. Ownership is the only other distinction the inputs draw (POL-NOTE-001/002).
- **Recommended: B** — two roles only: *a Notes-screen user* and, where the story is about writing/reading one's own note, *the note's owner*. US-NOTE-007's role is *the module's integrator*, matching the MDL precedent ("a consuming module's integrator", US-MDL-004).

**PROPOSAL 4 — does SEC self-registration deserve a story?**
- Affects: US-NOTE-007, scope.
- Options: **A)** no story — it is a platform invariant, not a module need. **B)** a story traced to POL-NOTE-006. **C)** a story with "Traces: — (scope only)".
- Researched: option C is fatal here — contract C4.4 requires every `US` to trace ≥1 POL, so a scope-only story is a MAJOR finding in this profile even though the engine's §2 permits the form in general. Option A loses the only PRD-level trace of the module-registry's most consequential line: without registration in SEC's Module/Screen/Action registries the one screen is absent from the menu and from direct-link access (G2/G3). POL-NOTE-006 is precisely the policy that requires that registered screen and its permission set to exist.
- **Recommended: B** — one MEDIUM story traced to POL-NOTE-006, worded as a need (the screen must be reachable), never as a registration mechanism or an XM: `INT-C`/`INT-R` stay empty-but-present per ADR-FIN-001.

Nothing else in the three inputs left a story's scope, priority or role ambiguous; the six policies are covered without any deferred story.
